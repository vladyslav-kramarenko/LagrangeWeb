What is Lagrange interpolation?

Lagrange interpolation constructs a polynomial that passes through a set of points with distinct X coordinates. Enter your known values above to calculate the polynomial and plot its curve. For example, the points (1, 2), (2, 5), and (3, 10) lie on f(x) = x² + 1.
